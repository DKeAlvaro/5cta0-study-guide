Exam rules (study guide 2026-27): written exam = multiple choice + open exercises, 70 % of the grade, minimum 5.0. **Allowed: one A4 sheet with your own notes, front and back, typed or handwritten, and a scientific or graphic calculator.** Exam Q1 2026-27: 26 October 2026; resit: January 2027. Every exam is marked out of 10.

## The ranking (effort = points × how often × how hard)
