Every past exam question, and the ranking that built this guide#

9 sittings · 2019 to 2026~150 sub-questionsThe spec of this guide

Exam rules (study guide 2026-27): written exam = multiple choice + open exercises, 70 % of the grade, minimum 5.0. Allowed: one A4 sheet with your own notes, front and back, typed or handwritten, and a scientific or graphic calculator. Exam Q1 2026-27: 26 October 2026; resit: January 2027. Every exam is marked out of 10.

The ranking (effort = points × how often × how hard)#

Score = total points over all sittings × fraction of sittings where the type appears × difficulty (1 = lookup, 2 = several steps, 3 = derivation with calculus). The guide spends effort in this order.

Out of scope (and why)#

  • jan19-4 Wiener FIR filter (2.5 p): Wiener filtering was dropped from the course content (Jan 2022 key, 2c: "no longer part of the content"). One line in U9.
  • jan20-4c/4d Gauss–Newton iteration (1.5 p): asked once, 2020; never since the course moved to the 2021 format. Summarised in three lines in U9.
  • nov19-1a cross-correlation from plots (1 p): the official key says the printed answer is wrong ("no correct answer, closest is B"). Solved in U3 with the correct values.
  • oct21-2a MVUE condition to pdf (1 p): asked once; the practice exam of 2021 was written by a different team. Sketched in U8, not drilled.
  • oct21-1f t-statistic (0.5 p): not in the current reader. Answered in U19 in two lines.
